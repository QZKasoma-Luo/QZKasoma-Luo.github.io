Wuthering Waves 4.0 is almost here. I thought we were supposed to get the Dreamland map
first, so why are we going straight to the New Federation? I still have a soft spot for
秧秧·玄翎, 穗穗, and 清宵. Before the new version lands, I want to praise these three properly.

## 秧秧

Honestly, I didn't feel much for 秧秧 at first. At launch she was the girl who picked the
Rover up in Jinzhou: gentle, soft-spoken, helping me register, taking me to meet Jinhsi, and
then... nothing. Once the main story moved to Rinascita, she disappeared from the plot. All
that was left were the letters she sent now and then. Everyone joked that she was "the poster
girl with the least screen time", and I laughed along.

The original 秧秧 was purely a letter-writer. I didn't read a single one (sorry). And every
letter came with a stingy handful of Shell Credits. Who even needs Shell Credits?

But then her SP form showed up in Dreamland, and that line — "…long time no see, Rover" —
hit me straight in the heart. Black feathers spread, the Xuanbird circles around her, and she
is a completely different person from the soft ranger. After all those letters, she finally
came to see me in person. How was I supposed to resist?

## 穗穗

Then there's 穗穗, 秧秧's older sister. I want both sisters, thank you very much.

Blonde hair, folding fan, polite in every sentence — and clearly someone who has already
calculated the whole ledger in her head. Not someone to cross. When 秧秧 went missing in the
story, 穗穗 was the one who went looking. Usually 秧秧 is the one taking care of others; this
time someone was worried about her. Maximum older-sister energy.

She is also genuinely good in combat. Put her next to 秧秧 and she is the older sister
covering the rear: buffs and healing. My usual team is 秧秧, 千咲, and 穗穗, and every fight
feels like the two sisters heading out to beat someone up. I wrote her into a fanfic before,
too; that dignified, slightly sharp way she talks is very easy to write.

## 清宵

<figure class="post-figure">
  <img src="/images/blog/before-4-0/qingxiao.jpg" alt="清宵 with long blue hair, holding a long jade weapon, seated against a mountain landscape with birds and cherry blossoms" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>清宵 · official character art · © KURO GAMES</figcaption>

清宵 is different from the other two. I like her purely because of how she plays.

I pulled her in 3.6 and got her first Resonance Chain, which was lucky. At first I played her
terribly: two energy bars to manage, switching too early, ult at the wrong time — the whole
rotation was a mess. For a while I wondered if I had pulled the wrong character. I thought I
was getting a cool, aloof swordswoman; instead I got a natural airhead.

So I kept practising. Gradually it clicked. One day the whole rotation finally connected —
the setup, her entrance, the burst, all in one go — and I actually shouted from my chair.
That kind of joy isn't something you get just by pulling a character; you earn it bit by bit.
That's why I feel a little differently about her, like a disciple I raised, or a partner I
went through hard times with.

## So, about 4.0

Please finish the Dreamland story first! 秧秧's background still hasn't been explained, and
now we're running off to the New Federation. I refuse to accept this.

That said, if the new map has good-looking characters, I'll probably end up saying "真香"
anyway. (Quietly.)
